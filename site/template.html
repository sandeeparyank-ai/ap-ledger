      How the ledger is kept

      Sources

      Results announcements and annual reports from SEC EDGAR and SGX, fetched twice a week in results months.

      The line

      The narrowest marketing line each company discloses. A&P where it exists, then sales and marketing, then selling and distribution. The type is shown on every entry.

      Like for like

      Changes are worked out in the reporting currency against the same period a year earlier, using restated comparatives where the filing gives them.

      Checked

      Every figure and quoted reason is matched word for word against the filing before it appears here. "Not disclosed" counts as a finding.

      Summaries and links only. Figures are as reported by each company and are not investment advice.
