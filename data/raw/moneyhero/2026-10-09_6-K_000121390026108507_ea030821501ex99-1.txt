MoneyHero Group Announces Departure of Chief
Commercial Officer Amid A New Market-Based Team Structure

Hong Kong/Singapore, October 9, 2026 (ET)

– MoneyHero Limited (NASDAQ: MNY) (“MoneyHero” or the “Company”), the largest AI-powered personal finance
aggregation and comparison platform and a digital insurance brokerage provider in Greater Southeast Asia, today announced the departure
of its Chief Commercial Officer, Shravan Thakur, effective December 22, 2026.

This comes at a time when MoneyHero has adopted
a country manager-led model that puts local leadership at the center of commercial execution and marketing activities across its four
markets — Hong Kong, Singapore, Taiwan and the Philippines. Under this model, MoneyHero has localized its marketing teams, enabling
them to work more closely with local commercial teams. Each country manager now leads both functions to develop unique product and marketing
offerings, to better serve their user communities and drive their market’s profitability. As such, the Company will not appoint
a replacement Chief Commercial Officer.

This new structure empowers country managers with
clear ownership over local priorities, uniting commercial and marketing activities under a single market leader. Since its introduction,
there has been much positive feedback from our users and partners to Danny Leung, Interim Chief Executive Officer and Chief Financial
Officer, indicating that this new leadership structure is beginning to bear fruit in both commercial execution and marketing activities.
Put simply, response to market has become faster and engagement with customers and partners has become tighter in every one of the four
markets.

Steven Teichman, Chairman of the Board
,
commented, “On behalf of the Board, I would like to thank Shravan for his contributions to MoneyHero and wish him every success
in his future endeavors. Our strategic priorities remain unchanged. Under Danny’s leadership, our teams across the Group remain
focused on executing those priorities, strengthening business performance and delivering long-term value for our customers, partners and
shareholders.”

The Board continues its search for a permanent
Chief Executive Officer. The Company will provide further updates as appropriate.

About MoneyHero Group

MoneyHero Limited (NASDAQ: MNY) is a leading
tech- and AI-powered personal finance aggregation and comparison platform that provides consumers with actionable insights to
discover, compare, and choose the best financial products with confidence — bringing data intelligence and seamless digital
access across insurance and banking solutions. The Company operates in Singapore, Hong Kong, Taiwan and the Philippines. Its brand
portfolio includes B2C platforms MoneyHero, SingSaver, Money101, Moneymax and Seedly, as well as the B2B platform Creatory.
MoneyHero had over 280 commercial partner relationships as at June 30, 2026, and had approximately 3.7 million Monthly Unique Users
across its platform for the three months ended June 30, 2026. The Company’s backers include Peter Thiel—co-founder of
PayPal, Palantir Technologies, and the Founders Fund—and Hong Kong businessman, Richard Li, the founder and chairman of
Pacific Century Group. To learn more about MoneyHero and how the innovative fintech company is driving APAC’s digital economy,
please visit www.MoneyHeroGroup.com.
