E. Notes to the condensed interim consolidated financial statements

1.   Corporate information

     F J Benjamin Holdings Ltd (the “Company”) is a limited liability company incorporated and domiciled in
     Singapore and is listed on the Singapore Exchange Securities Trading Limited (“SGX-ST”). These condensed
     interim consolidated financial statements as at and for the six months ended 31 December 2025 comprise
     the Company and its subsidiaries (the “Group”).

     The principal activities of the Company are those of investment holding and the provision of management
     services to its subsidiaries. The principal activities of the subsidiaries are those of investment holding and
     retailers, distributors and exporters of consumer fashion wear, accessories, timepieces, beauty, health and
     wellness products and operator of a multi-label luxury shoe and lifestyle concept store.

2.   Basis of preparation

     The condensed interim consolidated financial statements for the six months ended 31 December 2025
     have been prepared in accordance with SFRS(I) 1-34 Interim Financial Reporting issued by the Accounting
     Standards Council Singapore. The condensed interim consolidated financial statements do not include all
     the information required for a complete set of financial statements. However, selected explanatory notes
     are included to explain events and transactions that are significant to an understanding of the changes in
     the Group's financial position and performance since the last annual consolidated financial statements of
     the Group for the year ended 30 June 2025.

     The accounting policies and methods of computations adopted for the condensed interim consolidated
     financial statements are consistent with those adopted by the Group in its most recently audited annual
     consolidated financial statements for the year ended 30 June 2025, which were prepared in accordance
     with SFRS(I)s, except for the adoption of new and amended standards as set out in Note 2.1 below.

     The condensed interim consolidated financial statements are presented in Singapore dollar which is the
     Company's functional currency and all values in the tables are rounded to nearest thousand ($’000), except
     as otherwise indicated.

     2.1 New and amended standards adopted by the Group

     A number of amendments to the standards have become applicable for the current reporting period. The
     Group did not have to change its accounting policies or make retrospective adjustments as a result of
     adopting these standards. The adoption of these standards did not have any material effect on the
     financial performance or position of the Group and the Company.

     2.2 Use of judgements and estimates

     In preparing the condensed interim consolidated financial statements, management has made
     judgements, estimates and assumptions that affect the application of accounting policies and the reported
     amounts of assets and liabilities, income and expense. Actual results may differ from these estimates.

     The significant judgements made by management in applying the Group's accounting policies and the key
     sources of estimation uncertainty were the same as those that applied to the most recently audited annual
     consolidated financial statements of the Group as at and for the year ended 30 June 2025.

     Estimates and underlying assumptions are reviewed on an on-going basis. Revisions to accounting
     estimates are recognised in the period in which the estimates are revised and in any future periods
     affected.

3.   Seasonal operations

     The Group's businesses are not affected significantly by seasonal or cyclical factors during the financial
     period reported on.

4.   Segment and revenue information

     For management purpose, the Group is organised into business units based on their channel of distribution
     and has three reportable operating segments as follows:

     i.   The Ongoing Retail segment is involved in the operation of retail stores specialising in the retail of
          consumer fashion wear, accessories, timepieces, beauty, health and wellness products, chocolates
          and cafe.
     ii. The Distribution segment is involved in the distribution of consumer fashion wear, accessories, home
          furnishings, timepieces, beauty and health and wellness products.
     iii. The Export segment is involved in the export of consumer fashion wear, accessories, timepieces and
          health and wellness products.

     These operating segments are reported in the manner consistent with internal reporting provided to
     management who are responsible for allocating resources and assessing performance of the operating
     segments.

7.   Discontinued operations

     On 5 September 2025, the Company announced that its wholly owned subsidiary, F J Benjamin
     (Singapore) Pte Ltd, and Guess Europe Sagl had mutually agreed to end their relationship for the
     Group’s retail and distribution of all Guess products in Malaysia and Indonesia.

     The retail and distribution of Guess products were carried out by the Group’s subsidiary in Malaysia, F
     J Benjamin (M) Sdn. Bhd., and its associated company in Indonesia, PT Gilang Agung Persada.

     As at 31 December 2025, the Group’s Guess business in Malaysia was classified as a disposal group held
     for sale and as a discontinued operation. Accordingly, it is presented under discontinued operations
     while the Group's Guess business in Indonesia is presented under share of results of associates. The
     Group expects the handover of the Malaysia Guess stores and related assets to the new distributor to
     be completed in the second half of FY2026. In addition, the Group expects to continue recognising
     losses arising from discontinued operations in relation to the Group’s Guess business in Malaysia and
     the Group’s share of results of associates from the Group’s Guess business in Indonesia, in the second
     half of FY2026.

8.   Loss per share

     The basic loss per share is calculated by dividing the loss for the respective financial periods attributable
     to equity holders of the parent by the weighted average number of ordinary shares outstanding during
     the respective financial periods.

     Diluted loss per share is calculated by dividing the loss for the respective financial periods attributable to
     equity holders of the parent by the weighted average number of ordinary shares outstanding during the
     respective financial periods plus the weighted average number of ordinary shares that would be issued on
     the conversion of all the dilutive potential ordinary shares into ordinary shares.

4.   Review of performance of the Group

     (i)   Review of Results for the Six Months ended 31 December 2025

           On 5 September 2025, the Company announced that its wholly owned subsidiary, F J Benjamin
           (Singapore) Pte Ltd, and Guess Europe Sagl had mutually agreed to end their relationship for the
           Group’s retail and distribution of all Guess products in Malaysia and Indonesia.

           The retail and distribution of Guess products were carried out by the Group’s subsidiary in
           Malaysia, F J Benjamin (M) Sdn. Bhd., and its associated company in Indonesia, PT Gilang Agung
           Persada.

           As at 31 December 2025, the Group’s Guess business in Malaysia was classified as a disposal
           group held for sale and as a discontinued operation. Accordingly, it is presented under
           discontinued operations while the Group's Guess business in Indonesia is presented under share
           of results of associates.

           Continuing Operations
           Group revenue for continuing operations for the six months ended 31 December 2025 (“1H
           FY2026”) declined by 32.3% to $12.7 million, compared to $18.8 million in the previous
           corresponding period (“1H FY2025”), as a result of store closures and cessation of exports to
           Indonesia.

           Sales in Singapore (excluding exports to the Indonesian associate) declined by 45.1% year-on-
           year, primarily due to the closure of underperforming stores and continued cautious consumer
           sentiment. The strengthening of the Singapore dollar further weighed on domestic spending as
           consumers increasingly travelled and made purchases overseas. In contrast, sales in Malaysia
           increased marginally by 0.3% year-on-year, supported by growth in e-commerce and clearance
           sales, as well as contributions from new stores.




                                                 14
       In line with the decline in overall sales, gross margin declined by 3.0 percentage points which led
       to a drop in gross profit from $9.2 million in 1H FY2025 to $5.8 million in 1H FY2026. This is
       mainly attributable to deeper discounts and promotional activities undertaken for clearance
       sales.

       Other income mainly comprised market support and administrative service income as well as
       government grants received. The decrease was primarily due to the absence of write-back of
       payables and waiver of royalties in 1H FY2026.

       Group operating expenses decreased by 19.0% or $2.5 million to $10.6 million in 1H FY2026,
       from $13.1 million in 1H FY2025. Staff costs declined by 13.5% and rental expenses by 3.7%,
       primarily due to store closures and headcount reduction, partially offset by the introduction of
       8% Sales and Service Tax on the rental and services in Malaysia with effect from 1 July 2025.
       Advertising and promotion expenses were reduced by 41.7%, depreciation of furniture, fixtures
       and equipment by 48.6%, depreciation of right-of-use assets by 40.1% due to expired leases and
       other operating expenses by 14.7%. These reductions were attributable to the Group’s ongoing
       streamlining initiatives aimed at reducing operating costs.

       Interest expense on borrowings increased due to higher borrowings, while interest expense on
       lease liabilities decreased following the reduction in right-of-use assets.

       The Group’s share of loss from its Indonesian associate amounted to $1.9 million in 1H FY2026,
       compared to $0.9 million in 1H FY2025. Although the associate’s turnover increased by 1.4%
       year-on-year in local currency terms, deeper discounting implemented to stimulate demand
       resulted in a gross margin compression of approximately 8.1%, leading to a higher loss for the
       period. Of the $1.9 million share of loss recognised in 1H FY2026, approximately $1.0 million was
       attributable to losses arising from the termination of the Guess business, while the remaining
       $0.9 million arose from the associate’s underlying operating performance.

       There were no exports from Singapore to the Indonesian associate in 1H FY2026 as purchases
       were made directly by the associate in Indonesia.

       As a result of the above, the Group reported a net loss after tax from continuing operations of
       $7.6 million in 1H FY2026, compared to $4.9 million in 1H FY2025.

       Discontinued Operations
       The loss after tax from discontinued operations amounted to $1.9 million in 1H FY2026,
       compared to a profit after tax of $0.5 million in 1H FY2025.

       Overall, the Group reported a net loss attributable to shareholders of $9.5 million in 1H FY2026,
       compared to $4.4 million in 1H FY2025.

(ii)   Balance sheet

       Furniture, fixtures and equipment decreased by $0.4 million, mainly due to the transfer of assets
       to held for sale amounting to $1.3 million and depreciation charges of $0.4 million, partially
       offset by capital expenditure of $1.4 million.

       Right-of-use assets decreased by $1.7 million, arising from the transfer of assets to held for sale
       of $3.7 million and depreciation charges of $2.5 million. These were partially offset by the
       recognition of renewed and new leases amounting to $4.7 million. Correspondingly, total lease
       liabilities (current and non-current) decreased by $1.9 million, as payments of lease liabilities
       amounted to $2.8 million, offset by renewed and new leases and interest charges totalling $4.6
       million.

       Inventories decreased by $10.8 million, mainly due to lower purchases following store closures
       and the transfer of inventories to assets held for sale amounting to $2.3 million.
