VINFAST
ANNOUNCES LEADERSHIP TRANSITION; NAMES MR. PHAM NHAT QUAN ANH AS CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER

September
14, 2026 – VinFast Auto Ltd. (“VinFast” or the “Company”) announced a leadership transition in its executive
management team, effective September 12, 2026.

The
Board of Directors has appointed Mr. Pham Nhat Quan Anh as Chief Executive Officer of the Company, succeeding Mr. Pham Nhat
Vuong. Mr. Pham Nhat Vuong will continue to serve as a member of the Board of Directors. Concurrently, Mr. Pham Nhat Quan Anh has been
appointed Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of VinFast Vietnam JSC. The transition is intended to bring fresh perspectives and renewed
momentum to support VinFast’s continued evolution, operational excellence and long-term growth.

Mr.
Pham Nhat Quan Anh currently serves as Chairman of the Company. He also serves as Chief Executive Officer of VinMetal Trading and Production
JSC, an affiliate of the Company within the Vingroup ecosystem specializing in high-quality steel production. Since joining VinFast in
February 2019, he has held several senior leadership positions across vehicle development, manufacturing, sales and after-sales services,
including Vice Chairman and Standing Deputy General Director of VinFast Vietnam JSC, Deputy General Director of Global Sales, Marketing
and After-sales, and Director of the Planning, Program Coordination and Quality Inspection Division at VinFast Trading and Production
JSC before assuming his current leadership roles. Throughout his tenure with VinFast, he has been instrumental in supporting VinFast’s
rapid development and expansion from its initial localization phase in Vietnam to its global strategy.

Prior
to joining VinFast, Mr. Anh served as Deputy General Director and Deputy Chief Operating Officer of Vinpearl Joint Stock Company from
2017 to 2019, where he gained extensive experience in operational management and strategic planning across the hospitality and services
industries. Mr. Anh holds a Bachelor’s degree in business management from Singapore Management University.

Mr.
Pham Nhat Quan Anh commented: “I am deeply honored by the Board of Directors’ trust and confidence in entrusting me with
these additional responsibilities. Built on a strong foundation established over the years and supported by the growing confidence of
customers across our markets, VinFast is presented with a significant opportunity to help lead the global transition to green mobility.
I am committed to working closely with our talented team to uphold VinFast’s core values and continue delivering world-class products
and the highest standards of service to customers around the world.”

The
Company would like to express its appreciation to Mr. Pham Nhat Vuong for his leadership as Chief Executive Officer and looks forward
to his continued contributions as a member of the Board of Directors.
