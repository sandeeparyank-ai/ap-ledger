Ngoc Hoi Acquisition

● | On
September 15, 2026, VinFast Vietnam Joint Stock Company (“VFVN”), a subsidiary of the Company, entered into agreements to
acquire 100% of the equity interests in Ngoc Hoi Real Estate Investment Joint Stock Company (“Ngoc Hoi”) from Mr. Pham Nhat
Vuong, VinFast’s founder (“Mr. Pham”), and two other minority shareholders. Ngoc Hoi is a real estate development company
in which Mr. Pham owns a majority interest and which holds a 20% economic interest in an investor consortium developing the Hanoi International
Sports Urban Area Project, a large-scale integrated urban development project in Hanoi, Vietnam.

● | The
aggregate consideration was VND30,857 billion (approximately US$1.2 billion), representing a discount to the fair value of the transferred
shares as determined by an independent third-party valuation. The acquisition was completed in the third quarter of 2026. The consideration
was funded through a combination of existing financial arrangements with Vingroup Joint Stock Company (“Vingroup”) and Mr. Pham, together with a portion of the proceeds
from Mr. Pham’s capital contribution of VND20,000 billion (approximately US$793.5 million) to VFVN through VFVN’s issuance
of dividend preference shares that carry no conversion, exchange or similar rights, and has been fully settled. The acquisition is intended
to generate additional earnings and cash flows from real estate development, while the Company’s electric vehicle and smart mobility
businesses remain its core business and principal strategic focus.

● | Total
revenues were VND30,340.9 billion (US$1,203.7 million) in the second quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 32.9% from the

● | Vehicle
sales were VND28,833.1 billion (US$1,143.9 million) in the second quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 33.4% from the first

Cost of Sales and Gross Margin 

● | Cost
of sales was VND49,392.2 billion (US$1,959.5 million) in the second quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 36.1% from the first
quarter of 2026 and an increase of 148.0% from the second quarter of 2025. The increase compared to the first quarter of 2026 and second
quarter of 2025 was primarily attributable to an increase in the cost of vehicles sold as the Company delivered more EVs to customers.

● | Gross
loss was VND19,051.3 billion (US$755.8 million) in the second quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 41.7% from the first quarter
of 2026 and an increase of 435.4% from the second quarter of 2025.

● | Gross
margin was negative 62.8% in the second quarter of 2026, compared to negative 58.9% in the first quarter of 2026 and negative 21.8%
in the second quarter of 2025. The decrease in gross margin from the second quarter of 2025 was primarily attributable to (i) the recognition
of additional expenses related to the extended free charging program 12 launched in
February 2026, which was recorded as a deduction of revenue beginning in the first quarter of 2026, and (ii) revenue deferrals on certain
vehicle sales. The decrease in gross margin from the first quarter of 2026 was primarily attributable to revenue deferrals on certain
vehicles sales.

sales for which revenue has been deferred. As a result, these costs were recorded as selling expenses rather than as a reduction of revenue,
as they would have been if the revenue had been recognized.

● | Net
other operating expenses were VND960.7 billion (US$38.1 million) in the second quarter of 2026, representing an increase in net operating

driven by higher foreign exchange losses.

Business Outlook

● | As we progress through the second half of 2026, we continue
to navigate industry-wide semiconductor supply constraints affecting automotive manufacturers globally. VinFast has taken proactive actions
to strengthen supply visibility, including long-term procurement commitments extending through 2027 and direct engagement with senior
leadership teams at key suppliers to secure priority support for our production requirements. While we remain attentive to the evolving
supply environment, our current expectations for production and deliveries remain in line with our business plan, and we are maintaining
our full-year 2026 guidance.

● | VinFast’s
target markets are Vietnam, Indonesia, the Philippines, India, the Middle East, North America and Europe, and the Company continues to
evaluate opportunities to expand into additional countries and regions across Europe, Asia, the Middle East and Africa. VinFast aims
to grow its global footprint in markets where it identifies strong long-term EV adoption potential. This expansion strategy is aligned
with Vingroup’s broader global development approach, leveraging the Vingroup ecosystem, partnerships and capital resources to support
VinFast’s international growth.

● | VinFast
may establish or enlarge its presence in existing and new markets through a range of distribution models, with dealership partnerships
representing the predominant approach in recent periods. To ensure sufficient production capacity to support its current and future operations,
VinFast intends, from time to time, to expand its international manufacturing capacity, assembly and distribution capacity across its
markets, including through the expansion of existing facilities as well as new brownfield and greenfield projects. While VinFast remains
focused on the mini- through E-segments of the electric SUV market, it continues to evaluate a broader spectrum of vehicle types for
future product development. VinFast regularly reviews its global expansion strategy and retains flexibility to accelerate, moderate or
otherwise adjust its distribution, manufacturing, assembly, marketing and other market plans as conditions evolve.

● | VinFast’s
funding sources for its capital requirements to implement its growth strategy are expected to include loans, grants, and transactions
with its major shareholders and affiliates, as well as public and private debt and equity capital markets, third-party borrowings and
cash from operations. VinFast also expects to opportunistically access the debt and equity capital markets from time to time, subject
to market conditions. The Company also seeks to capture available tax incentives, subsidies and other policy support programs to reduce
the cost of investment and operations.
